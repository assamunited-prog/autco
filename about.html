About AUTCO

AUTCO — 15 Years of Building, 26 Years of Experience

AUTCO is a resourceful Omani contractor with its own equipment, plant and machinery — executing prestigious projects with leading consultants and architects across the GCC.

“Built on a reputation of quality — and the repeat business that proves it.”
Company Profile

Who We Are

AUTCO LLC has the technical expertise right from formulating preliminary design concepts to drafting our own recommendations from preliminary design data or sketches. Our expertise embraces the full range of services in commercial and industrial installations and maintenance, along with the ability to procure and purchase the materials and equipment required for any project.

We work competitively using our coordination expertise on electrical and plumbing services, coupled with an experienced engineering team whose philosophy brings the highest possible level of expertise, professional involvement and individual commitment to each project undertaken. Each completed project has exceeded customer satisfaction, because AUTCO is built on a reputation of quality.

We also operate a transport business, moving raw materials, steel, ceramics and goods across the GCC and to different places of Oman.
